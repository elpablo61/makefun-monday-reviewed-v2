One line. A review of my own Monday. Nothing else.

> Monday, 11:20. Agus from finance approved my expense report in 12 seconds, then spent an hour asking why I bought a mouse in March.

`main.ts` writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).
The line is a single line, under 200 characters, with a named person, a specific
time, and one concrete scene — calibrated against verified top-10 make.fun
winning lines: a micro-anecdote that sounds true, no joke, no explaining.
